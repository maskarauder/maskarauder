# Maskarauder

Open-source desktop audio, phone routing, and livestream utilities — **[maskarauder.com](https://maskarauder.com/)**.

## Projects

- **[MicDrop](https://maskarauder.com/micdrop/)** ([source & releases](https://github.com/maskarauder/MicDrop)) — Stream desktop audio to an Android phone or iPhone and send your phone or headset microphone back over local Wi-Fi/LAN (public beta for Windows 11 & Android; experimental Linux x64 & Apple Silicon macOS 14.2+ hosts; iPhone on TestFlight).
- **[Twitch Subathon Timer](https://maskarauder.com/#timer-project)** ([source & releases](https://github.com/maskarauder/subathon-timer)) — Local Python countdown utility that listens to Twitch EventSub WebSockets (subs, Bits, Channel Points) and updates an OBS `Text (GDI+)` source over OBS WebSocket.
- **[YouTube Playlist Player](https://github.com/maskarauder/yt-player)** — Local `uv` + Chrome automation script that rotates YouTube playlists and restarts OBS streams cleanly on schedule.
- **[Slippy2 Desktop](https://github.com/maskarauder/slippy2-desktop)** — Independent, detached fork of Slime2 Desktop for local OBS browser-source chat overlays.

## Browser Audio Tools (Local, No Upload)

Free in-browser sound checks on **[maskarauder.com/tools](https://maskarauder.com/tools/)**:

- **[Online Microphone Test](https://maskarauder.com/tools/microphone-test/)** — Live digital dBFS level meter, room-noise vs. speech comparison, and local 10-second clip playback.
- **[Left / Right Stereo Headphone Test](https://maskarauder.com/tools/stereo-test/)** — Channel isolation, spoken voice labels, polarity phase check, and mono fold-down test.
- **[Online Tone & Pink Noise Generator](https://maskarauder.com/tools/tone-generator/)** — Bounded sine waves, logarithmic frequency sweeps, and pink noise for speaker and EQ checks.
- **[Discord, OBS & VB-CABLE Audio Routing Helper](https://maskarauder.com/tools/audio-routing/)** — Interactive signal-path diagrams and checklists for Windows 11 virtual cable setups.
- **[Local WAV File Inspector](https://maskarauder.com/tools/wav-inspector/)** — Inspect sample rate, bit depth, channels, clipping, and peak dBFS locally without uploading files.
- **[Audio / Video Sync & Headphone Lag Check](https://maskarauder.com/tools/audio-sync-test/)** — Flash-and-click timing loop with adjustable millisecond offset for Bluetooth and OBS monitoring delay.
